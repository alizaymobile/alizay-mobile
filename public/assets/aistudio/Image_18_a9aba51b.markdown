## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, premium omnichannel tech storefront engineered specifically for South Asian consumer electronics retail. The identity merges contemporary consumer tech minimalism with high-velocity retail utility: crisp digital canvas surfaces, immediate pricing transparency, and tactical contrast.

### Core Philosophy
- **Uncompromised Trust & Authenticity:** Tech retail in competitive regional markets demands undeniable credibility. High-contrast typography, clear warranty signals, and structured product provenance dismantle buyer friction.
- **Speed & Precision:** Mobile-first architecture tuned for rapid device comparisons, instant specs scanning, and seamless WhatsApp or direct checkout pathways.
- **Curated Premium Tone:** Avoids discount-bazaar clutter; utilizes balanced whitespace, disciplined slate structural lines, and focused jewel-tone conversion accents.

### Design Style: Modern Precision Commerce
A hybrid of crisp Corporate Modern and tactile e-commerce utility. Key characteristics:
- Pristine `#ffffff` primary surfaces paired with foundational `#f8fafc` layered shelves.
- Ultra-sharp hairline dividers in slate to segment dense technical specifications.
- Deep slate-black structural anchor points (`#0f172a`) for authoritative headlines and high-value status counters.
- Energetic emerald and cyan accents acting strictly as conversion catalysts and authenticity indicators.

## Colors

The palette balances clean surgical tech hardware aesthetics with reliable commercial trust.

### Color Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#059669` Emerald Green):** The primary brand catalyst. Applied to primary calls-to-action (Buy Now, Add to Cart), stock availability badges ("In Stock"), official warranty seals, and positive status states.
- **Secondary (`#0d9488` Deep Teal/Cyan):** Supporting retail action accent. Applied to secondary conversion targets, promotional tags (e.g., "Installment Available"), trade-in flags, and WhatsApp quick-connect triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#0f172a` Midnight Slate):** Anchor tone for dominant product headings, price figures, tab bar active states, and administrative control panels.
- **Neutral (`#64748b` Cool Slate):** Governs secondary specifications, SKU metadata, deactivated states, and helper instructions.

### Foundation & Canvas Tokens
- **Canvas Base:** `#ffffff` for core product spotlight cards and modal panels.
- **Canvas Substrate:** `#f8fafc` for general page backgrounds and spec sheet matrices.
- **Hairline Structural Borders:** `#e2e8f0` for crisp boundary isolation without visual weight.
- **Price Strikeout / Subtle Text:** `#94a3b8`.
- **System Error / Destructive:** `#e11d48` (Admin Delete, Out of Stock, Order Canceled).

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a clean, modern, and readable tech retail interface.

### Editorial Guidelines
- **Product Titles:** Set in `headline-md` or `title-sm` with `fontWeight: 600` or `700` in Midnight Slate (`#0f172a`). Never use pure black (`#000000`).
- **PKR Currency Typography:** 
  - Standard format: `Rs. 249,999` or `PKR 249,999`.
  - Prefix ("Rs." / "PKR") must be set at `fontWeight: 600` with neutral slate coloring (`#64748b`) or emerald green (`#059669`).
  - Numerical value must be set using `currency-display` with `fontWeight: 800` and tight letter spacing (`-0.01em`).
- **Specification Matrix:** Memory, RAM, storage, and chipset details use `body-sm` with tabular numerical alignment to allow vertical comparison between phones.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptable fluid grid with strict hardware-like vertical rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout, `margin: 1rem` (16px), `gutter: 0.75rem` (12px). Product lists render in a 2-column compact grid.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `margin-md: 1.5rem` (24px), `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Product listings adapt to 3 columns; administrative inventories transition into split master-detail views.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, max-width `1280px`, `margin-lg: 2.5rem` (40px), `gutter-lg: 1.5rem` (24px). Product listings render across 4 columns; administrative consoles use persistent left-hand navigation.

### Rhythmic Rules
- Product card interiors use strict internal padding of `space-md` (16px) on mobile and `space-lg` (24px) on featured modules.
- Administrative tables and inventories prioritize vertical compression (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) for density, with explicit row dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface layering and ambient, ultra-diffused drop shadows rather than heavy elevation.

### Hierarchy Tiers
- **Level 0 (Floor):** Base canvas `#f8fafc`. Completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shelf):** Surface `#ffffff` framed by a 1px solid hairline border in `#e2e8f0`. Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Product Tiles):** Lifted surface `#ffffff` with border `#cbd5e1`. Shadow: `0px 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0px 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Bars & Cart Triggers):** Surface `#ffffff` with 90% opacity and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Border: `#e2e8f0`. Shadow: `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals, Action Sheets & Toasts):** Surface `#ffffff` completely opaque, with high-def ambient diffusion: `0px 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The system uses `roundedness: 2` (base radius of `0.5rem` / 8px). This creates a contemporary, ergonomic tech-product feel while maintaining clean, engineered corners for tables and hardware galleries.

### Geometric Mapping
- **Product Tiles & Cards:** Standard `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for an approachable, sleek consumer feel.
- **Buttons & Input Fields:** Base `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) to reflect tactile control surfaces.
- **Tags, Official Badges & Stock Indicators:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast against rectangular device aspect ratios.
- **Bottom Navigation / Floating Tab Bar:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for handheld thumb clearance.

## Components

### 1. Product Cards
- **Structure:** White container (`#ffffff`), `rounded-lg` (16px), 1px border `#e2e8f0`.
- **Image Frame:** Fixed aspect ratio (1:1 or 4:5), `#f8fafc` backdrop with `object-contain`.
- **Badge Slot:** Top-left positioned pill badge (e.g., "Official Warranty", "PTA Approved"). Top-right wishlist or compare action trigger.
- **Pricing Cluster:** Displays current price formatted as `Rs. [Amount]` in `headline-md` (`#0f172a`), with original price crossed out in `#94a3b8` `body-sm`. Installment sub-text in `#0d9488` `label-sm`.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Buy / Add to Cart):** Background `#059669`, text `#ffffff`, `fontWeight: 700`, `rounded: 0.5rem`. Hover: `#047857`.
- **Secondary Retail Action (WhatsApp Order / Inquiry):** Background `#f0fdf4`, border 1px solid `#bbf7d0`, text `#15803d`, `fontWeight: 600`.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, text `#0f172a`. Hover: `#f8fafc`.
- **Admin Destructive (Delete Product):** Background `#fff1f2`, border 1px solid `#fecdd3`, text `#e11d48`. Hover: `#ffe4e6`.

### 3. Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Small, pill-shaped tags.
  - *PTA Approved / In Stock:* Surface `#ecfdf5`, border `#a7f3d0`, text `#065f46`.
  - *Non-PTA / Pre-Order:* Surface `#fffbeb`, border `#fde68a`, text `#92400e`.
  - *Out of Stock:* Surface `#f1f5f9`, border `#e2e8f0`, text `#64748b`.
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive `#ffffff` with border `#e2e8f0`. Active `#0f172a` with text `#ffffff`.

### 4. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Fields:** Surface `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`, text `#0f172a`, `rounded: 0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state: border `#059669` and `ring: 2px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`.
- **Currency Field Prefix:** Integrated un-editable block showing `PKR` or `Rs.` with `#f1f5f9` background and `#475569` text.

### 5. Floating Bottom Navigation (Mobile)
- Positioned floating 16px above screen bottom, margin inline 16px.
- Glassmorphic surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, border 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, `rounded-xl` (24px).
- Active item utilizes primary emerald tint `#ecfdf5` with `#059669` icon and text.

### 6. Modal Sheets & Drawers
- Mobile cart and filter sheets pull up from the bottom with an explicit pill drag handle (`#cbd5e1`, 36px x 4px).
- Desktop quick-view modals center on screen, max-width 640px, framed by 1px `#e2e8f0` border and Level 4 shadow.

### 7. Alert Toasts
- Surface `#0f172a` (dark anchor toast) with crisp `#ffffff` text, or status-tinted containers with left-hand 4px solid status borders (`#059669` success, `#e11d48` error).

### 8. Admin Controls (Upload, Edit, Delete)
- **Top Bar Utility Strip:** High-contrast bar in `#ffffff` with border-b `#e2e8f0` containing bulk actions, quick inventory search, and "Upload New Device" button (`#059669`).
- **Product Row Table:** Alternating clean hover row states, inline edit inputs for price/stock, and explicit icon-based triggers (Edit: `#0f172a`, Delete: `#e11d48`).
